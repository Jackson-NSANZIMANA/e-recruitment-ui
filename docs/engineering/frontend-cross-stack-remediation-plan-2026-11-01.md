## 2. D8 backend: forensics → identity → edge

The inspection target drops revision and the closed-document reason in intermediate projections; the edge safe set also maps the observed upstream reason to a contract mismatch. Neither observed behavior nor a proposed 409 is an approved citizen contract.

**Backend deliverables.** Forensics owns the revision/closed state source; identity owns complete and safe propagation; edge owns the public allowlisted mapping; application-service remains the only lifecycle authority. The packet must settle success fields, types and nullability, upload/retry and revision meaning, and the public HTTP status/body for a closed document. It must state whether and under what authority further upload is refused; it must not introduce an unapproved reopen path. Revision alone is not evidence that a retry is byte-identical.

**Required red-to-green full-chain test, backend-owned:** run a forensics → identity → edge request through the actual controllers/adapters and serialization boundaries, with deterministic fake or live backing dependencies explicitly identified. Upload a synthetic document and assert that the exact authoritative revision, not a guessed value, survives both hops to the edge response in the approved shape. Exercise a document in the authoritative closed state through the same chain; assert the **approved** refusal status and safe body arrive at the citizen edge, not a success, generic 502 or leaked upstream diagnostic. Verify no new revision/state mutation on refusal. Include absent/malformed revision and unrecognized upstream error fail-closed cases, authorization/agency isolation, and approved retry/idempotency behavior. Preserve response traces and test revision tied to the approved SHA. Fake-backed integration demonstrates wiring, not infrastructure readiness; repeat the approved path with real services and required infrastructure before operational acceptance.

**FE acceptance:** using controlled edge responses first, test approved revision display/handling, closed refusal and unknown/malformed success; no fabricated revision, silent reopen, false success or data leakage. Run end-to-end through the approved deployed edge after backend tests pass. A UI mock is not evidence of forensics propagation.

## 3. D10 backend: lane semantics and safe SoD projection

Application-service owns the transaction, agency isolation, verified principal and lifecycle. Edge owns the public projection. The inspected application code may emit a SoD rule but the edge currently reduces the observed upstream 403 to bare `FORBIDDEN`. A frontend must never infer SoD from an ordinary 403.

**Contract decisions and deliverables:** the application and edge owners, security and integration authority must agree and publish the allowed lane values; whether and when omission is permitted; the outcomes for multiple held lanes with omission, a syntactically invalid lane, a valid but not-held lane, and a stale hold; permitted decisions per lane and expected refreshed queue. Reconcile application result, edge status/body, OpenAPI, handoff and client packet, including the inspection target's shared `ADJUDICATION_LANE_REQUIRED` mapping for some different conditions. Do not promise distinct codes or UI actions until approved. Agree an explicit, minimal safe SoD discriminator and which authenticated caller may see it; forbid arbitrary upstream message, actor identifiers, evidence or internal data from crossing the edge.

**Required tests:**

1. **Application transaction (fake-backed plus live DB):** actor A makes the relevant earlier human decision; actor A then attempts the prohibited later decision in the same agency and receives the authoritative SoD refusal with no mutation or duplicate decision. An authorized distinct actor B follows the approved path; wrong agency, missing permission and unauthenticated principals remain refused. Test isolation and concurrent/stale decisions against a real database as well as unit fakes.
2. **Edge safe projection:** drive the actual edge route and upstream adapter with a controlled application-service SoD 403 containing the authoritative rule plus synthetic secret diagnostic, actor ID and unrelated fields. Assert exactly the approved status and allowlisted outcome survive, while all arbitrary fields and messages are absent from response and relevant logs. An ordinary permission 403 and unknown rule must not masquerade as SoD; unknown/malformed upstream payload fails closed. Also exercise approved lane refusals and held-lane projection through gateway serialization. Run this edge test at the approved SHA; add a live full-chain SoD test before release. An edge fake alone cannot prove database separation of duties.
3. **Lane reconciliation:** cover single and multiple holds, omitted, invalid, valid-but-not-held and stale lanes, explicit decision targeting, no unintended lane updates, and refreshed state under concurrency. Assert the approved semantics at transaction, gateway and client boundaries; prevent automatic retries of a decision.

## 5. D11 identity and walk-in credential: fail-closed tests

**D11 backend:** IAM/edge and field-sync owners must agree a safe authenticated officer subject and prove it derives from the verified principal, not a browser-provided identifier; publish canonical route and signed-capture actor-binding rules. Test valid binding, missing/forged/mismatched actor, revoked/expired session, wrong agency and replay on the real signed path. Define how captures made under an old session or by another officer are retained/quarantined/disposed, without rewriting attribution. **FE:** until this exists, do not fabricate an officer UUID, submit under another actor, or silently reattribute old captures. Test missing identity, principal change, old capture and approved `OFFICER_MISMATCH` handling; no sync on uncertainty. The current inspected physical route does fabricate a local actor: this plan does not change it, and the affected flow must remain blocked from release pending remediation. D11 is a release blocker, not a warning.

**Walk-in backend/authority:** decide whether the credential is opaque server-verified or another approved format, how it is presented and resolved, trusted issuance, binding to correct application/agency/venue/day where applicable, expiration/revocation/supersession, walk-in admission and physical score rules. Specify what is and is not authenticated without inventing reservation or slot claims. **Conditional future integrated test:** using only the approved representation and approved validation, create synthetic walk-in → vet → issue → present → server verify/admit where required → physical score → authoritative lifecycle result through the real edge and services. Verify expected applicable wrong agency/venue/day, altered, malformed, expired, revoked or superseded credentials fail closed and do not cause a score/state change; cover scheduled-invitation regression and RNP/RCS policy. Never manufacture or decode claims the approved contract does not provide; avoid logging bearer material. The parser rejection test is an independent safety check and is not a requirement that opaque codes be accepted by a structured-token parser.

## 6. Fail-closed contract tooling

**FE platform deliverables, after the SHA/packet gate:**

1. Produce a versioned, exhaustive operation ledger, joined to the approved backend packet and actual imports/routes/surfaces. Classify each registry entry as **actually used**, **alias**, **composed operation** or **registry-only** (additional tags may coexist). An alias records its canonical backend operation and all actual consumers; a composed read records every underlying backend operation and projection. A registry-only entry is not claimed active, but must have explicit owner, rationale and disposition; undeclared actual usage such as `uploadMyDocument` must fail surface completeness. Detect additions, removed callers, duplicate/ambiguous aliases, stale compositions and unclassified entries automatically rather than relying on file-wide decoder heuristics.
2. For each applicable operation prove **request**, **success response** and **outcome/error** coverage independently against the exact approved source-reconciled schemas and runtime policy (path, method, auth, permission, CSRF, retry, and agency where applicable). Explicit `no body`, `no response body` or `no declared domain outcome` classifications require evidence from the packet and tests, not a blank cell. Map decoders to actual call sites and prove runtime enforcement of malformed successes and unknown outcomes, rather than merely finding a decoder in the same file. Aliases and compositions must cover both the canonical wire contract and their consumer-specific transformations.
3. Missing mappings, unknown dimensions, malformed schemas, stale/unapproved classifications, unrecognized operations, expired waivers and SHA/packet/source mismatch must make `verify:fe` nonzero. The current Gate E notes (including uncovered D10 request/outcome) become actionable failures, not silent notes. A reviewed, time-limited exception may apply only to an explicitly classified **registry-only** entry with no reachable consumer: record owner, reason, linked issue, approver and expiry; expiry or newly reachable use fails the gate. No exception can mark an active uncovered contract green or waive a security/release blocker. A verification report lists exceptions separately and never calls them coverage.
4. Add negative self-tests that deliberately remove each request/response/outcome mapping, add an unclassified operation, omit an alias/composition source, make a registry-only entry reachable, expire an exception, change the checkout SHA, inject malformed 2xx and unknown errors, and violate declared auth/CSRF/retry policies; each must fail for the intended reason. Add positive fixtures for genuinely absent bodies/outcomes and approved alias/composition mappings. CI must run these self-tests and the complete gate on the pinned checkout, publishing coverage by category and dimension.

## 7. Exit criteria and proof boundaries

FE-P0 stays **open** until: owners and authority approve the SHA and packet; all affected contract dimensions are reconciled and implemented against that SHA; D8 full-chain, D10 transaction and safe edge projection, D11 verified identity, walk-in credential, runtime decoders and fail-closed tooling pass their required tests; browser and live integration evidence are recorded; and remaining blockers are explicitly resolved. Retain agency isolation, narrowly authorized evidence access and the distinction between evidence and decisions.

Keep `OFFLINE_CAPTURE_CAN_SYNC=false`: S15 encrypted storage, restart/replay, conflicts and reconciliation proof are prerequisites for durable offline synchronization. The pilot is online-first with rehearsed supervised paper fallback. Do not authorize real recruitment selection decisions without defensible evidence and agency, DPO, legal, security and operational sign-offs. Autonomous ranking, ungoverned cross-agency search/dashboard, biometric matching and native/offline scope remain deferred; geographic venue optimization is separate from S09/S14. Report unit, fake-backed, live-infrastructure, mocked browser E2E and real operational rehearsal evidence separately; feature code or a mocked test alone is not operational proof.
